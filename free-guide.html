About Memory Bridge Books

Memory Bridge Books creates therapeutic coloring books specifically designed for seniors with dementia and Alzheimer's disease. Our collections focus on historically accurate, nostalgic themes that spark memories and meaningful conversations.

Unlike generic adult coloring books, every design is crafted with bold outlines, high contrast, and period-authentic details to support therapeutic engagement—especially for people in mid-to-late stage dementia.

We created these books—and this guide—because we believe in the dignity and humanity of every person experiencing memory loss, and the caregivers who love them.
