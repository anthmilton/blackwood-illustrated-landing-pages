Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Memory Bridge Books ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information in connection with our website (memorybridgebooks.com), our products, and our use of third-party services including the Pinterest API.

This policy complies with applicable privacy laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other relevant regulations.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Website Visitors

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and access times
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, links clicked, and navigation patterns
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device (see Section 7 below)

2.2 Customers and Purchasers

When you purchase our products through third-party platforms (Amazon, Etsy), those platforms handle payment processing and order fulfillment. We do not directly collect or store payment information. Please review the privacy policies of:

  • Amazon Privacy Notice
  • Etsy Privacy Policy

2.3 Pinterest Users

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from Pinterest users. Our use of the Pinterest API is strictly limited to managing our own business account.

3. Use of Pinterest API

We use the Pinterest API solely for the following purposes:

  • Posting our own original educational content (coloring book pages and related materials) to our Pinterest business account
  • Managing our own Pinterest boards and pins
  • Accessing basic analytics about our own posted content only
  • Scheduling and organizing content publishing
Important: We do not:
  • Access, collect, or store information about other Pinterest users or their activities
  • Track Pinterest user behavior or interactions with our pins
  • Store any data obtained through the Pinterest API (beyond campaign analytics for our own account)
  • Share or sell any Pinterest-sourced information with third parties
  • Use Pinterest data for advertising targeting outside of Pinterest

4. How We Use Information

Information we collect from our website may be used to:

  • Provide and improve our website and services
  • Understand how visitors use our site
  • Respond to customer inquiries and support requests
  • Send marketing communications (only with explicit consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate our website (hosting, analytics)
  • E-commerce Platforms: Amazon and Etsy (when you purchase our products)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

We do not share any Pinterest-sourced data with third parties, including other advertising services or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain website analytics data for up to 24 months. We do not retain any Pinterest API data except for campaign analytics related to our own account. You may request deletion of your information at any time (see Section 10).

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertising (only with consent)

8. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website and Pinterest content may link to third-party services including:

  • Amazon (product sales)
  • Etsy (digital downloads)
  • Pinterest (social sharing)

These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies:

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Privacy Notice
  • Etsy Privacy Policy

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. While our coloring books may be used by or with children (particularly in memory care, educational, or family settings), all transactions and online interactions are conducted with adults (parents, guardians, caregivers, or educators).

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at the address below, and we will delete such information.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format (GDPR)
  • Do Not Sell: California residents can opt out of data sales (we don't sell data)

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@memorybridgebooks.com

11. International Data Transfers

If you are accessing our services from outside the United States, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

12. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Secure API credential storage
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security audits

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Compliance with Pinterest Developer Guidelines

Our use of the Pinterest API complies with Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and Developer and API Terms of Service. We:

  • Only access information with proper authorization (OAuth tokens)
  • Do not store Pinterest user data (except campaign analytics for our own account)
  • Do not combine Pinterest data with other data sources
  • Do not share or sell Pinterest-sourced data with third parties
  • Maintain secure API credentials with restricted access
  • Act transparently about our API usage and its purposes
