Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 12, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Memory Bridge Books ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with our use of the Pinterest API to share educational coloring book content.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from Pinterest users or visitors. Our use of the Pinterest API is limited to:

  • Posting our own original educational content (coloring book pages and related materials)
  • Managing our own Pinterest boards and pins
  • Accessing basic analytics about our own posted content

How We Use the Pinterest API

We use the Pinterest API solely to automate the posting of our educational coloring book content to our Pinterest business account. This includes:

  • Creating and publishing pins featuring our coloring book pages
  • Organizing content into boards by category (memory care, fishing, gothic, kawaii, etc.)
  • Scheduling posts for optimal reach

We do not access, collect, or store information about other Pinterest users or their activities.

Data Storage and Security

We do not store any personal data from Pinterest users. Our API credentials and posting automation are secured using industry-standard practices. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

Third-Party Services

Our website and Pinterest content may link to third-party services (such as Amazon KDP, Etsy, or our own product pages). Those services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. While our coloring books may be used by or with children (particularly in memory care and educational settings), all transactions and interactions are conducted with adults.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

For information about how Pinterest handles data, please review Pinterest's Privacy Policy.
